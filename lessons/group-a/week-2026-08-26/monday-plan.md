# Group A — Monday — The Hook & Concept

**Week of:** 2026-08-26
**Level:** 8 (City Explorer)
**Topic:** Places in the city
**Grammar Focus:** "There is / There are" (addressing the Spanish 'hay'). + WH - Questions to explore the city!
**Group:** Kids, Age 8
**Mode:** Advance

---
## 00-05m | The Hook
Quick warm-up: flash city building picture cards and students shout 'There is a ___!' before the buzzer.
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing today's vocabulary on the board.

## 05-18m | The Discovery
Draw a city map on the board with buildings. Model 'There is a bank' / 'There are two parks' for different amounts.
Target grammar: **"There is / There are" (addressing the Spanish 'hay'). + WH - Questions to explore the city!**.
This week's vocabulary: 🗽 Statue  ·  🛣️ Street  ·  🌉 Bridge  ·  🏙️ Tower  ·  🏫 School  ·  📍 There Are
Ask: Which question word do we use to ask about a place — where or what?

## 18-35m | The Sandbox
Students take turns asking a partner 'Where is the ___?' using their drawn map.

## 35-50m | The Boss Level (preview)
Short preview activity to build anticipation for Friday's boss challenge: "The Mayor is testing new recruits on their knowledge of the city before granting patrol badges."
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after the first student volunteers to try the new structure aloud.

## 50-60m | The Cool Down
Quick recap, tally points, say goodbyes.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — if the whole group nails the concept-check by the end of class.
