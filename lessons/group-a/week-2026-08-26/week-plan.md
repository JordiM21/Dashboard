# Group A — Week of 2026-08-26

**Level:** 9 (How Much?)
**Topic:** money
**Grammar Focus:** Some / Any
**Group:** Kids, Age 8
**Mode:** Advance

---
## Monday — The Hook & Concept

### 00-05m | The Hook
Quick warm-up: sort a pile of food picture cards into two magic baskets — 'Countable' and 'Uncountable' — as a class race.
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing today's vocabulary on the board.

### 05-18m | The Discovery
Draw a wizard's pantry on the board with countable items (apples, eggs) in one jar and uncountable items (water, rice) in another. Model 'How many apples?' vs 'How much water?' for each.

Target grammar: **Some / Any**.
This week's vocabulary: 🥕 Carrot  ·  🍎 Apple  ·  🥄 Some  ·  🥚 Egg  ·  🙋 Want  ·  🧪 Need
Ask: Can you count eggs one by one? So do we use 'many' or 'much'?

### 18-35m | The Sandbox
Students draw their own pantry with 3 countable and 3 uncountable items, labeling each with 'How many' or 'How much'.

### 35-50m | The Boss Level (preview)
Short preview activity to build anticipation for Friday's boss challenge: "It's Market Day in the enchanted village and the shopkeeper only understands correctly formed quantity questions."
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after the first student volunteers to try the new structure aloud.

### 50-60m | The Cool Down
Quick recap, tally points, say goodbyes.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — if the whole group nails the concept-check by the end of class.

## Wednesday — Deep Practice

### 00-05m | The Hook
Fast recap game of Monday's vocabulary and grammar target.

### 05-18m | The Discovery
Recap: countable things (apples, eggs) use 'How many' + plural noun. Uncountable things (water, rice) use 'How much' + singular noun. Blue = countable, Red = uncountable.
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing the rule.

### 18-35m | The Sandbox
Heavy fill-in-the-gaps practice. Students complete the sentences on the whiteboard:
1. Is there ________ (some/any) sugar left?
2. How ________ (much/many) water is in the bottle?
3. There aren't ________ (some/any) eggs in the basket.
4. He needs ________ (some/any) carrots for the rabbit.
Students match a food card to 'some' (positive) or 'any' (question/negative) speech bubbles.

### 35-50m | The Boss Level
Each student picks one real food item and asks the class the correct quantity question about it.
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after each student's turn.

### 50-60m | The Cool Down
Review answers, tally points, celebrate progress.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — when the group completes the hardest gap-fill correctly.

## Friday — Boss Day

### 00-05m | The Hook
Party-day welcome — quick recap game, high energy, review the full week's vocabulary. Split the class into "🦉 Owl Traders" and "🦊 Fox Traders".
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — announcing today's Boss Challenge.

### 05-18m | The Discovery
It's Market Day in the enchanted village and the shopkeeper only understands correctly formed quantity questions.

### 18-35m | The Sandbox
Teams prepare for the Boss Challenge — quick roleplay rehearsal using this week's vocabulary.

### 35-50m | The Boss Level
Market Roleplay: students visit the shopkeeper's stall and must ask for items using 'some', 'any', 'how much', or 'how many' correctly to 'buy' the card.
Win condition: The team that fills their basket with 5 items first wins Market Day.
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before each team's final answer.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — when a team beats the Boss Challenge.

### 50-60m | The Cool Down
Final scoreboard, victory sounds, goodbyes.
[TRIGGER: Applause] — for every student, regardless of the final score.
