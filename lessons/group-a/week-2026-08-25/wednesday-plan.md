# Group A — Wednesday — Deep Practice

**Week of:** 2026-08-25
**Level:** 6 (Right Now)
**Topic:** Present Continuous (I am playing)
**Grammar Focus:** Differentiating "I play" (routine) vs "I am playing" (now)
**Group:** Kids, Age 8
**Mode:** Advance

---
## 00-05m | The Hook
Fast recap game of Monday's vocabulary and grammar target.

## 05-18m | The Discovery
Board recap: 'I cook every day' (routine) vs 'I am cooking now' (happening right now).
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing the rule.

## 18-35m | The Sandbox
Heavy fill-in-the-gaps practice. Students complete the sentences on the whiteboard:

1. ________ (It/He) is boiling — look at the bubbles!
2. I ________ (cook/am cooking) pasta right now.
3. We usually eat pizza, but today we ________ (eat/are eating) pasta.

Drag-and-drop: match each cooking verb to its -ing form.

## 35-50m | The Boss Level
Each student mimes a cooking action and says the full sentence: 'I am ___ing.'
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after each student's turn.

## 50-60m | The Cool Down
Review answers, tally points, celebrate progress.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — when the group completes the hardest gap-fill correctly.
