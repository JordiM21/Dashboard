# Group A — Monday — The Hook & Concept

**Week of:** 2026-08-25
**Level:** 6 (Right Now)
**Topic:** Present Continuous (I am playing)
**Grammar Focus:** Differentiating "I play" (routine) vs "I am playing" (now)
**Group:** Kids, Age 8
**Mode:** Advance

---
## 00-05m | The Hook
Play 'Live Kitchen': the teacher acts out a cooking action right now and students describe it in real time.
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing today's vocabulary on the board.

## 05-18m | The Discovery
Draw a giant pasta pot on the board with steam. Model 'It is boiling right now' vs 'We boil water every day' to contrast routine and now.
Target grammar: **Differentiating "I play" (routine) vs "I am playing" (now)**.
This week's vocabulary: 🍝 Pasta  ·  👨‍🍳 Chef  ·  🥣 Bowl  ·  🍝 Eat  ·  👅 Taste  ·  ⏰ Now
Ask: What do we add to the verb when something is happening right now — nothing, -s, or -ing?

## 18-35m | The Sandbox
Students draw themselves cooking and label the action: 'I am ___ing.'

## 35-50m | The Boss Level (preview)
Short preview activity to build anticipation for Friday's boss challenge: "Live Pasta Cook-Off! The judges want a play-by-play of what each chef is doing right now."
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after the first student volunteers to try the new structure aloud.

## 50-60m | The Cool Down
Quick recap, tally points, say goodbyes.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — if the whole group nails the concept-check by the end of class.
