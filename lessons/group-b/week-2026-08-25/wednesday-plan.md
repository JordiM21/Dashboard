# Group B — Wednesday — Deep Practice

**Week of:** 2026-08-25
**Level:** 9 (How Much?)
**Topic:** Uncountable vs. Countable nouns
**Grammar Focus:** Some / Any.
**Group:** Young Teens, Age 13
**Mode:** Advance

---
## 00-05m | The Hook
Fast recap game of Monday's vocabulary and grammar target.

## 05-18m | The Discovery
Board recap: 'There are some potions.' (countable, plural) vs 'There is some gold.' (uncountable, no plural).
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing the rule.

## 18-35m | The Sandbox
Heavy fill-in-the-gaps practice. Students complete the sentences on the whiteboard:

1. She wants ________ (some/any) XP before leveling up.
2. We don't have ________ (some/any) arrows left.
3. How ________ (much/many) gems dropped from that chest?
4. He needs ________ (some/any) swords for the final fight.

Students match a resource card to 'some' (positive) or 'any' (question/negative) speech bubbles.

## 35-50m | The Boss Level
Each student picks one real item from their backpack or pockets and asks the class a correct quantity question about it.
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after each student's turn.

## 50-60m | The Cool Down
Review answers, tally points, celebrate progress.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — when the group completes the hardest gap-fill correctly.
