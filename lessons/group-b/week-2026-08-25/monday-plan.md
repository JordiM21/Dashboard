# Group B — Monday — The Hook & Concept

**Week of:** 2026-08-25
**Level:** 9 (How Much?)
**Topic:** Uncountable vs. Countable nouns
**Grammar Focus:** Some / Any.
**Group:** Young Teens, Age 13
**Mode:** Advance

---
## 00-05m | The Hook
Play 'Loot Reveal': the teacher reveals a resource icon piece by piece and students guess it, then classify it as countable or uncountable.
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing today's vocabulary on the board.

## 05-18m | The Discovery
Draw a game inventory screen on the board with countable items (potions, swords) in one slot and uncountable resources (gold, XP) in another. Model 'How many potions?' vs 'How much gold?' for each.
Target grammar: **Some / Any.**.
This week's vocabulary: 🏹 Arrows  ·  🍗 Food  ·  💎 Gems  ·  ⚔️ Swords  ·  🧪 Potions  ·  📦 Drop
Ask: Do we say 'How many gold' or 'How much gold'? Why?

## 18-35m | The Sandbox
In pairs, students sort resource cards into 'countable' and 'uncountable' inventory slots drawn on the board and justify each choice.

## 35-50m | The Boss Level (preview)
Short preview activity to build anticipation for Friday's boss challenge: "The final boss just dropped a legendary loot crate — but the vault only opens for correctly-asked quantity questions!"
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after the first student volunteers to try the new structure aloud.

## 50-60m | The Cool Down
Quick recap, tally points, say goodbyes.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — if the whole group nails the concept-check by the end of class.
