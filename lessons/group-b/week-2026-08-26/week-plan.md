# Group B — Week of 2026-08-26

**Level:** 10 (Rule Maker)
**Topic:** School rules
**Grammar Focus:** Must / Mustn't and Should / Shouldn't
**Group:** Young Teens, Age 13
**Mode:** Advance

---
## Monday — The Hook & Concept

### 00-05m | The Hook
Play 'Coach Says': students only follow the action if the teacher says 'You must ___.'
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing today's vocabulary on the board.

### 05-18m | The Discovery
Introduce a rookie character who needs advice — model 'You should ___' / 'You shouldn't ___' as students help.

Target grammar: **Must / Mustn't and Should / Shouldn't**.
This week's vocabulary: 🏕️ Camp  ·  🚫 Mustn't  ·  ⚔️ Third-Party  ·  🎒 Loot  ·  💊 Heal  ·  📢 Communicate  ·  🔄 Rotate
Ask: What's the difference between 'should' and 'must'?

### 18-35m | The Sandbox
In pairs, students create three survival rules using must/mustn't.

### 35-50m | The Boss Level (preview)
Short preview activity to build anticipation for Friday's boss challenge: "The Coach is hosting Strategy Trivia Night before the tournament."
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after the first student volunteers to try the new structure aloud.

### 50-60m | The Cool Down
Quick recap, tally points, say goodbyes.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — if the whole group nails the concept-check by the end of class.

---

## Wednesday — Deep Practice

### 00-05m | The Hook
Fast recap game of Monday's vocabulary and grammar target.

### 05-18m | The Discovery
Board recap: 'You mustn't camp in the open' (rule) vs 'You shouldn't forget to reload' (advice).
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before revealing the rule.

### 18-35m | The Sandbox
Heavy fill-in-the-gaps practice. Students complete the sentences on the whiteboard:
1. You ________ (must/mustn't) stay in the storm — it deals damage every second.
2. ________ (It/We) must check the map before every rotation.
3. Players ________ (must/mustn't) heal up before pushing the next zone.
Drag-and-drop: match each rule to 'must' or 'mustn't.'

### 35-50m | The Boss Level
Each student states one survival rule using must/mustn't.
[TRIGGER: Applause] — after each student's turn.

### 50-60m | The Cool Down
Review answers, tally points, celebrate progress.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — when the group completes the hardest gap-fill correctly.

---

## Friday — Boss Day

### 00-05m | The Hook
Party-day welcome — quick recap game, high energy, review the full week's vocabulary. Split the class into "✅ Team Must" and "🚫 Team Mustn't".
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — announcing today's Boss Challenge.

### 05-18m | The Discovery
The Coach is hosting Strategy Trivia Night before the tournament.

### 18-35m | The Sandbox
Teams prepare for the Boss Challenge — quick roleplay rehearsal using this week's vocabulary.

### 35-50m | The Boss Level
Strategy Trivia Showdown: the host reads a scenario and teams must decide must/mustn't or should/shouldn't correctly.
Win condition: The team with the most correct answers wins Strategy Trivia Night.
[TRIGGER: Drumroll] — before each team's final answer.
[TRIGGER: Confetti + Victory] — when a team beats the Boss Challenge.

### 50-60m | The Cool Down
Final scoreboard, victory sounds, goodbyes.
[TRIGGER: Applause] — for every student, regardless of the final score.
